I am currently a research intern working on 3D surface reconstruction under the supervision of [Dr. Jiancheng (JC) Yang](https://jiancheng-yang.com/) at Aalto University. My research interests include **3D Computer Vision** and **AI for Science & Health**, particularly in the field of Human-AI Collaboration and Medical Image Processing. 

I hold a BSc in Math and an MSc in Computer Science. My thesis work, supervised by [Dr. Chris Child](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/about/people/academics/chris-child), focused on building environment models for reinforcement learning tasks. The work was published in the [proceedings of ISNN 2025](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-95-1233-1_3). After graduating, I was also mentored by [Dr. Giacomo Tarroni](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/about/people/academics/giacomo-tarroni) at CitAI Research Centre, where I worked on VLM-guided methods for dataset partitioning and processing in federated computer vision pipelines.
